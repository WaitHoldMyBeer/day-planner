# Playbook

Lessons the mentor has drawn from earlier runs. The routine agent applies lessons marked `active`
and ignores the rest. Only the mentor edits this file, under the rules in its prompt: a lesson
starts as `candidate`, needs evidence from two different days or from your own choices to become
`active`, and is retired when it stops helping. Every change is logged in `playbook-changelog.md`.

You can edit or delete any lesson. A line you write yourself counts as stated by the owner.
