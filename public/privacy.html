Day Planner privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-24

Day Planner is a small personal planning app run by its owner for themselves and people they invite. It is not a commercial service and it does not sell or share data.

What the app stores

  • Your account: the email address and name you enter, and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Your planner: the tasks and time blocks you create, and your color settings.
  • If you connect Google Calendar: the Google account email you connected and the OAuth tokens Google issues, stored encrypted, used only to read and change events on your primary calendar when you ask the app to.

How Google Calendar data is used

With your permission, the app reads events from your primary Google Calendar to show them on the day you are looking at, creates events when you choose "Add to Google", and deletes events when you choose "Delete from Google". Event data is fetched on demand and displayed to you; it is not stored on the server beyond the identifiers of events you import or create, and it is never shared with anyone else. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Use Disconnect in the Google menu to revoke the app's access and delete the stored tokens; you can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your account and planner entirely, ask the person who runs this instance.

Cookies

A single session cookie keeps you signed in. There is no advertising and no analytics tracking.
